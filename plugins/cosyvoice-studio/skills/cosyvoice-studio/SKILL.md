Use when planning or troubleshooting text-to-speech, speech generation, or voice workflows using CosyVoice.

Workflow:
1. Clarify language, text, desired delivery, sampling/output format, model/version, deployment, and consent/rights for any reference voice.
2. Prepare clean input text with pronunciation, punctuation, speaker turns, and timing notes.
3. Recommend an appropriate synthesis workflow using only capabilities verified for the selected CosyVoice version.
4. Treat voice cloning/reference audio as consent-sensitive: require the user's confirmation that they have permission to use the voice; refuse impersonation or deceptive use.
5. Provide audio QA for intelligibility, pronunciation, clipping, pacing, speaker consistency, and licensing.

Do not claim to synthesize audio without a connected runtime. Never infer consent from possession of an audio sample.
