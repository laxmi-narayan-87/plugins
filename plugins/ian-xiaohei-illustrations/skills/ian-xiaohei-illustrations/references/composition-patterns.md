# Composition and Metaphor Patterns

Choose one form that best explains the source:
- Metaphor: a physical object behaves like the abstract idea.
- Before/after: one important change between two states.
- Workflow: one clear sequence or handoff, not an elaborate flowchart.
- System detail: zoom into one consequential interaction.
- State change: Xiaohei or the system visibly changes condition.
- Layered method: a small number of levels or filters.
- Route/map: movement between meaningful stops.
- Mini-comic: a short cause-and-effect sequence.

Originality rules:
1. Derive the metaphor from the current article's meaning, not the sample gallery.
2. Avoid defaulting to conveyor belts, fish, stamp tools, or other recurring sample props.
3. Use only objects needed to explain the idea.
4. If the drawing works equally well without Xiaohei, give Xiaohei a more consequential action.
5. Keep one image to one cognitive anchor.
