# Style DNA

- Canvas: landscape 16:9, pure white.
- Line: thin black sketch lines with slight natural wobble, not vector-perfect.
- Composition: sparse, readable, generous negative space; main subject around 40–60% of canvas.
- Accent colors: use only a few red, orange, or blue handwritten notes.
- Text: short Chinese labels, usually fragments rather than sentences.
- Mood: uncanny, thoughtful, dryly humorous, clean, inventive; never childish or overly cute.
- Avoid: beige paper, grain, gradients, shadows, high-gloss 3D, stock-illustration polish, slide layouts, thick card borders, crowded labels, title banners, and decorative clutter.
