# Semantic fidelity

Protected literals are necessary but insufficient. Meaning also lives in relationships between people, actions, amounts, dates, negation, modality, causality, conditions, exceptions, scope, chronology and source attribution.

Never change:
- may improve to improves, or associated with to causes
- did not fail to failed
- some users to all users
- a reported observation to a proven fact

Use an explicit source-to-output comparison for subtle cases. Regex checks are not semantic understanding.
