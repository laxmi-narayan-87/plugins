# Not Ai brand rationale

The symbol combines a clean text-line motif with a small check-like edit mark: the writing remains recognizable while a specific revision is made. It avoids generic AI motifs and is designed to read in monochrome at small sizes.

- Light logo: dark ink on a warm white field.
- Dark logo: pale blue-white ink on a deep navy field.
- Composer icon: simplified page-and-edit mark, square, high contrast, no text.
