# Signs of slop

Audit only signs that are actually present, and record their location. Each sign is paired with a practical fix.

## Surfaces and color
- Purple/blue or violet/pink gradients across backgrounds, cards, buttons, or navigation → neutral surfaces and a restrained semantic accent.
- Gradient text, colored glows, radial washes, blurred blobs, neon borders, or glossy glass cards → solid text, quiet hairlines, surface steps, and meaningful color only.
- Too many near-identical text tints or every element highlighted → three clear text roles and one or two meaningful accent moments.

## Type
- Very heavy headings, excessive sizes/weights, all-caps tracked labels, negative tracking on small text, or italic emphasis → compact scale, sentence case, readable tracking, restrained weights.
- Inconsistent number formats → consistent formatting and tabular figures.

## Icons
- Decorative emoji, sparkles for generic AI, mismatched icon sizes/colors, or misaligned icon columns → one icon family and consistent geometry, or no icon when it adds no meaning.

## Spacing
- Arbitrary values, inconsistent sibling row/card dimensions, weak grouping, uneven padding, or unexplained gaps → consistent spacing scale and clear within-/between-group rhythm.

## Copy
- Greeting headlines, exclamation marks, hype words, vague placeholders, unsupported claims, unexplained metric deltas, or periods on short labels/buttons → direct product-specific language with verifiable claims.

## Components and data
- Excess badges, glowing pills, every control as a pill, decorative sparklines, rainbow charts, mini charts in every stat card, repeated same-size cards, or fake-looking data → quiet metadata, purpose-based radius, meaningful charts, hierarchy, and plausible data.

## Landing pages and visuals
- Generic headlines, stacked centered hero by default, too many CTAs, unverified social proof, floating blobs/orbs, stock robots/brains/circuits, text baked into generated imagery, or visuals unrelated to product → specific headline, one primary and at most one secondary CTA, product-relevant visual, or no image.

## Motion
- Pulse/ping on non-live elements, transition-all, hover scaling, bouncy springs, everything flying in, parallax, or layout-property animation → restrained purpose-driven transitions on opacity/transform/color and reduced-motion support.

## Template fallback
A cleaned screen can still look interchangeable. Look for a palette, headline, visual, or layout that could belong to any product. Let the product's actual purpose, user, data, and working environment define its character.
