# Social Content Planner

Turn a clear audience insight or verified research into a useful content plan.

## Workflow
1. Identify audience, platform, goal, desired action, voice, cadence, and constraints. Use known context rather than re-asking.
2. Check factual claims have evidence; use source verification when evidence is missing.
3. Generate distinct angles: tutorial, teardown, story, opinion, case study, checklist, and data-led insight.
4. Create specific hooks without misleading clickbait.
5. Draft for platform format and reading behavior; preserve creator voice and avoid generic AI phrasing.
6. Suggest visuals, proof points, CTA, and repurposing variants.
7. Arrange ideas into a calendar with date, channel, format, objective, and asset dependencies.

## Output
Campaign thesis, ranked concepts, ready-to-edit drafts, visual directions, CTAs, and calendar. Label assumptions and dates.

## Guardrails
Do not invent results, testimonials, statistics, or personal experiences. Do not publish or schedule content. Obtain explicit approval before any external posting action.
