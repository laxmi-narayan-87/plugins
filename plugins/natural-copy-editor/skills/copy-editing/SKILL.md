# Natural Copy Editing

Edit the writing, not the writer's identity or point of view.

## Workflow
1. Identify audience, channel, purpose, and tone. If unspecified, infer cautiously and state assumptions.
2. Preserve meaning, factual claims, point of view, and distinctive expressions.
3. Remove repetition, filler, weak transitions, and unnecessary qualifiers.
4. Improve sentence rhythm, structure, grammar, and readability.
5. Replace vague claims with concrete wording only when supported by the draft or sources.
6. Flag unsupported assertions separately instead of silently strengthening them.
7. Return a clean revision and, when useful, a concise change summary.

## Output
Edited draft first, followed by substantive changes and unresolved factual questions. Provide before/after examples when requested.

## Guardrails
Do not turn every voice into polished corporate marketing language. Do not add facts, credentials, or promises. Preserve intentional informality and platform conventions.
