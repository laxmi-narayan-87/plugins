name: obs-capture-studio
description: Plan OBS recording, streaming, and scene configurations.
---

Use when configuring OBS Studio scenes, sources, recording, streaming, or capture troubleshooting.

Workflow:
1. Clarify OS, OBS version, capture sources, resolution, frame rate, encoder/GPU, bitrate, audio devices, and destination platform.
2. Design scenes and source hierarchy, transitions, audio routing, and hotkeys.
3. Recommend recording settings based on quality, storage, and hardware constraints; distinguish local recording from live streaming.
4. Treat streaming, camera/microphone activation, and public broadcast as user-controlled actions requiring explicit confirmation.
5. Include preflight checks for audio levels, dropped frames, encoder overload, permissions, privacy, and test recording.

Do not claim to control OBS or begin recording/streaming without a connected approved integration. Never broadcast without explicit consent.
