Use when planning or troubleshooting lossless cuts, trimming, merging, and metadata-preserving media operations in LosslessCut.

Workflow:
1. Clarify input containers/codecs, desired cut points, output format, stream selection, and whether exact frame-accurate cuts are required.
2. Explain keyframe constraints and when a stream-copy cut may shift boundaries or require re-encoding.
3. Provide safe steps for marking segments, selecting tracks, exporting, and verifying outputs.
4. Before destructive edits or overwriting, request explicit approval and recommend preserving originals.
5. Validate duration, timestamps, audio/video sync, metadata, and playback across target players.

Do not promise frame-accurate lossless cuts where codec keyframes prevent them. Do not claim an export occurred without a verified runtime.
