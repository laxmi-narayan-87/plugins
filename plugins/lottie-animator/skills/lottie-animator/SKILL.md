description: Prepare lightweight vector animations for Lottie.
---

Use when designing, optimizing, reviewing, or handing off vector animations for Lottie players.

Workflow:
1. Determine source tool, target platform/player, dimensions, loop behavior, frame rate, and whether interactivity is required.
2. Recommend a vector-first scene structure and identify unsupported or risky effects for the chosen Lottie exporter/player.
3. Plan timing, easing, masks, shape layers, text handling, and asset dependencies.
4. Review the exported JSON or user-provided animation structure for size, unsupported features, naming, and performance.
5. Include platform validation steps for playback, scaling, looping, reduced motion, and fallback behavior.

Never claim to export or preview Lottie without connected software. Warn that player compatibility varies and request target player/version for precise advice.
