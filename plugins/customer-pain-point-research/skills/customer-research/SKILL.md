# Customer Research

Convert unstructured feedback into evidence-backed customer insight.

## Workflow
1. Establish product, target user, research question, and supplied corpus or source links.
2. Preserve source metadata: URL or record ID, date, platform, and context.
3. Clean duplicates and obvious spam without silently deleting ambiguous feedback; note exclusions.
4. Code feedback into themes: user goal, trigger, pain point, workaround, desired outcome, and severity.
5. Distinguish direct user statements from analyst interpretation. Include short attributed quotations.
6. Estimate frequency only when corpus supports it; state sample size and collection bias.
7. Prioritize needs by frequency, severity, urgency, and confidence.
8. Suggest opportunities and follow-up questions, not unsupported product prescriptions.

## Output
Research scope, sample limitations, theme table, evidence snippets, jobs-to-be-done, prioritized needs, and open questions.

## Guardrails
Do not invent quotes or personas. Do not generalize self-selected online samples to an entire market. Redact personal identifiers.
